The Mon Valley Independent recently highlighted CNX’s bat stewardship efforts at Monessen’s City Park, where employees installed a bat box to provide additional roosting habitat. The project brings together employee teamwork, wildlife conservation, and practical support for a local community. CNX employees built the seven-chamber box using Pennsylvania Game Commission plans and installed it September 4, along with three bluebird boxes.
