Continuing its annual support for local 4-H and FFA exhibitors, CNX supported eight fairs across western Pennsylvania and northern West Virginia this season, purchasing 31 exhibits at seven livestock sales. Those purchases recognized months of work by young people raising animals, with the livestock then processed and donated to help feed people in local communities. CNX’s support was unique to each fair.
