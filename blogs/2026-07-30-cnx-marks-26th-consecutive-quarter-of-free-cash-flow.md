CNX Resources Corporation reported another quarter of durable performance in the second quarter of 2026, generating $138 million in free cash flow and marking its 26th consecutive quarter of positive free cash flow generation. Since the inception of its seven-year plan in 2020, CNX’s differentiated business model has now produced approximately $3.1 billion in cumulative free cash flow. “Q2 2026 marks our 26th consecutive quarter of free cash flow generation, highlighting our Sustainable Business Model that is the cornerstone of our success,” said Alan Shepard, President and CEO.
