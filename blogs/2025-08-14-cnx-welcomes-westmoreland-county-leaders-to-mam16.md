By Positive Energy Hub StaffThis summer, CNX welcomed Westmoreland County Commissioners, County Public Safety officials, and Hempfield Township Chairman R. Doug Weimer to CNX's MAM16 well pad. The visit marked another step in CNX’s ongoing partnership with Westmoreland County.During the tour, guests learned more about CNX’s commitment to safety, responsible operations, and efficient, transparent natural gas production.
