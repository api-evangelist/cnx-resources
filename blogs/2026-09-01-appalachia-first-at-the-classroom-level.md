The energy workforce of the next decade is sitting in a classroom right now — in a shop class, in an auditorium with an HVAC system that works, in a cafeteria line where a student isn’t doing math on lunch debt. Preparing and inspiring that workforce — removing the obstacles that have nothing to do with how hard a student works — takes a lot of hands. Educators, school staff, PTAs, coaches, and families carry most of that weight.
