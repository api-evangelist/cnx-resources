This year, 52 graduates of the CNX Foundation’s Mentorship Academy committed to a next step: a job, an apprenticeship, or military service. On August 14, graduates gathered at PNC Park for Signing Day — the culmination of a program year spent touring job sites, meeting industry leaders, and building the professional network that turns an interest into an offer. These graduates are heading into roles with CNX, UPMC, Deep Well Services, BestPLUS, Sevita Health, apprenticeships with the building trades, and — for three graduates — into military service.
