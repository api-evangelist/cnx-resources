Water supply concerns are taken seriously across the region, and PA DEP Water Supply Investigation 394129 and 394137 offer clear examples of how these situations are addressed—from initial report through resolution. The situation In December 2025, CNX was notified of potential water supply reduction affecting two residences in Bell Township, Westmoreland County. Situations like this can arise for a variety of reasons, including natural fluctuations in groundwater availability.
