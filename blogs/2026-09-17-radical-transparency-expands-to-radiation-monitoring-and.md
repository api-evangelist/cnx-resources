CNX Resources has announced the next evolution of Radical Transparency, its first-in-the-nation public-private environmental monitoring and disclosure collaboration with Governor Josh Shapiro and the Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection (DEP). The program will now add continuous ambient gamma radiation monitoring at CNX’s unconventional well pads and certain produced water facilities, along with expanded public disclosure of radiation surveys on waste leaving its operating sites.
